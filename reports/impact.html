This report pairs those survey findings with two full fiscal years of statistics to tell the story of how York County uses its library.

Circulation

Patrons love checking out books — the numbers back it up

In our patron survey, borrowing books was rated the most important library service by every single age group — the highest scores across all four service categories (avg. 3.79–4.00 out of 5). It is the bedrock of everything we do.

Patrons highlighted frustration with hold waitlists and small in-branch collections — particularly at Fort Mill. "It is hard to browse books as the selection is small in person."

{# Gate Count #}
Library Visits

People are coming through the door — and coming back

92.3% of survey respondents visited YCL in the past year. Teens (12–18) lead at 57.1% weekly. Adults 25–40 follow at 49.7% weekly. Even seniors (65+) visit at 36–41% weekly.

The primary barrier for non-users is simply being too busy (17 of 42 non-users) — a scheduling gap, not a service-quality problem.

{{ stat_card('#1e8449','Gate Count',data.gate.v1,data.gate.v2,data.gate.pct,'Total patron visits counted at branch entrances.') }}
{# Programming #}
Programs & Events

Demand for programs far exceeds what our spaces allow

Children's programming is the most-requested service category — programs fill up immediately due to space limits. Working parents asked for evening and weekend storytime; a tween gap (ages 8–12) was flagged independently across dozens of responses.

61.2% of respondents attended at least one YCL signature event. The Summer Learning Challenge (272 selections) and Winter Reading Challenge (218) led all events.

{# Hotspots #}
Digital Access & Hotspots

Connectivity is a core library service — and demand is growing

Internet access and Wi-Fi ranked as the top technology service, leading for patrons 41+. YCL's hotspot lending extends connectivity into patrons' homes — a critical equity service.

50–64% of every age group uses the YCL app, including 59.3% of seniors, proving digital-first services work across generations.

{{ stat_card('#117a65','Hotspot Checkouts',data.hotspots.v1,data.hotspots.v2,data.hotspots.pct,'Total hotspot device checkouts, each representing home internet access.') }}
{# New Cards #}
New Library Cards

Every new card is a new member of the YCL community

New card registrations reflect YCL's reach into a fast-growing county. Word of mouth is the strongest non-website channel for teens and young adults — outperforming every digital platform.

The primary barrier for non-users is being too busy, pointing to scheduling convenience as the key lever for reaching new cardholders.

{{ stat_card('#784212','New Library Cards Issued',data.cards.v1,data.cards.v2,data.cards.pct,'Adult and juvenile new card registrations combined.') }}
{# Prints #}
Printing & Computer Access

Public technology access remains an essential equity service

Printing serves patrons without home office access — job applications, government forms, school materials. Survey respondents asked for more technology literacy programming (AI tools, computer basics, cybersecurity for seniors).

86–93% of every age group uses the YCL website; app adoption is strong and cross-generational.
