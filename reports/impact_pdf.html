YCL
What the Community Is Asking For Next
Open-ended survey responses  ·  430 analyzed  ·  April 2026

The same survey that validated YCL’s core services surfaced a clear signal about where the community wants the library to grow. The ten themes named most consistently across 430 independent open-ended responses — virtually every specific request ultimately runs into the same two structural constraints: buildings that are too small, and program hours that working families cannot reach.
