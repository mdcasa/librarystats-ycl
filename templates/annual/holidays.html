System-wide dates the library is closed or closes early. Full-day closures cost each branch its own normal hours for that weekday (set on the Weekly Hours page), so branches with different Friday/Saturday schedules lose different amounts of time on the same date. Partial closures (e.g. an early-closing day) use a flat hours-closed value applied to every branch. Either way, these feed into Section J "Hours Open" for whichever fiscal year the date falls in.
